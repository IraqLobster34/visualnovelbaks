В Suno включи **Instrumental**. В режиме **Custom** вставляй каждый промпт в поле **Styles**. [Инструкция Suno](https://help.suno.com/en/articles/3726721)

## «Служебный архив» · Главное меню

> Instrumental main menu theme for a visual novel about an internal investigation at a secret research facility. Chamber noir and dark ambient, 64 BPM, D minor. A quiet, recurring three-note motif D–F–E on felt piano, answered by a distant bowed cello harmonic. Warm tape texture, muted clockwork percussion, soft bass drone. Controlled, patient, faintly uneasy, with the warmth of an office light after hours. Give the motif a little space between repetitions. Establish an even level early and return to a similar texture near the end for a natural crossfade loop. No vocals, choir, trailer hits, or big finale.

## «Переговорная Б-4» · Заседание

> Instrumental dialogue underscore for a tense internal tribunal hearing in a sealed, windowless room. Slow-burn chamber noir, 58 BPM, D minor. Felted low piano states D–F–E only occasionally. Sparse bowed cello, deep room-tone synth, one muted wooden tick every few bars. Cold air, meticulously polite questions, pressure through silence. Steady and unobtrusive under long Russian dialogue, with a restrained midrange and smooth dynamics. Make the closing texture blend into the opening for a crossfade loop. No vocals, harsh high frequencies, jump scares, heavy drums, or dramatic swells.

## «Серая папка» · Материалы G-19 и торг

> Instrumental psychological thriller underscore for an investigator opening a sealed archive and the witness quietly turning the hearing around. 70 BPM, D minor. The D–F–E motif becomes a low prepared-piano ostinato with subtle rhythmic gaps. Add dry pizzicato cello, a narrow analog bass pulse and soft brushed metal. Increase tension gradually through texture and dissonance, keeping the level suitable under dialogue. Return to the spare opening pulse for looping. No voice, dialogue samples, risers, loud percussion, heroic chords, or climax.

## «Административное крыло» · Финал главы 2

> Instrumental end-of-chapter cue for a quiet, warmly lit government corridor after a closed hearing. 62 BPM, D minor. Bring back the D–F–E felt-piano motif an octave higher, with a soft bass clarinet and mellow tape haze. The immediate danger has passed, but the final harmony leaves the next move unresolved. Brief and spare arrangement, gently loosening over the final bars with a clean, quiet ending. No vocals, celebratory crescendo, dance beat, or emphatic final hit.
